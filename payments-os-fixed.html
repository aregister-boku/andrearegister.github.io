Assumptions & Sources

Every number this tool produces is derived from the parameters below. Values are benchmark estimates for directional decision-making — they are not rate quotes. Where a value is set by regulation or published network pricing, that basis is stated; where it is a blended practitioner estimate, it is labeled as such. Verify against your own PSP invoices, interchange qualification reports, and current network fee bulletins before committing spend. Last reviewed: July 2026. No data entered in this tool leaves your browser.

Forecast Calibration (Learn layer)

When measured outcomes are logged in Execution Tracking, all estimates are rescaled by factor = 1 + (actual ÷ forecast − 1) × n/(n+3), clamped to 0.5–1.5. The n/(n+3) weight shrinks small samples toward 100% so a single outlier cannot whipsaw the model; raw estimates are retained and recalibrated fresh each time (no compounding). This is a deliberately simple pooled estimator — it does not yet segment calibration by module or market.

Known Limitations

All inputs are self-reported — the tool has no connection to transaction data. Interchange is modeled as blended averages, not per-BIN qualification. UK–EEA and inter-regional consumer cross-border interchange are folded into the single cross-border premium. Local payment method economics are modeled at method level, not scheme level. Calibration is pooled across all modules. Treat outputs as a prioritization framework, not a forecast commitment.
