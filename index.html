The Problem This Work Solves

Most companies can see payment performance. Fewer can turn it into owned execution.

Authorization rate is an input. Billthrough is the metric that maps to collected revenue.

The recurring gap is ownership and prioritization.

Where revenue gets lost

Unclassified issuer declines
Retry timing misaligned to local behavior
Routing rules disconnected from economics
Payment method gaps by market or segment
No feedback loop from outcome to prioritization
System Architecture — Six Layers
Layer Function Output Business Impact
Ingest Unify PSP, issuer, retry, routing, lifecycle, method, and cost signals Normalized payment performance inputs Common source of truth across Product, Payments, Finance, and Engineering
Classify Separate terminal failures, recoverable failures, routing gaps, and method gaps Actionable recovery and optimization taxonomy Reduces noise and avoids wasted execution
Size Quantify recoverable revenue, margin impact, customer impact, and payback Economic opportunity sizing Turns payment performance into investment decisions
Rank Prioritize by ROI, confidence, complexity, dependencies, and capacity Ranked decision queue Roadmaps become economically defensible
Route Assign work across Product, Payments, Engineering, Finance, Commercial, and regions Owned execution path Reduces organizational drag between insight and action
Learn Measure realized impact, adoption, latency, overrides, and persistence Feedback loop for future recommendations System quality compounds over time
